This is a detailed story of preparing for GSoC in a short window while balancing uncertainty, deadlines, and skill gaps.

## What Helped Most

- Picking one target ecosystem and going deep
- Showing visible public progress through contributions
- Getting early feedback and iterating quickly

The biggest shift was moving from passive learning to shipping small but concrete work every day.
